# Movipiq – Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** October 27, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Movipiq ("the App") is a mobile application designed to help users discover movies, manage watchlists, record movie preferences, and receive personalized movie recommendations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and how users can request the deletion of their information. Where consent is required by applicable law, we obtain it separately. ## 2. Information We Collect Movipiq may process the following types of information to provide its services: **Account Information** - Email address - Unique user identifier - Account registration and authentication information **Movie Preferences** - Movies marked as liked - Movies marked as disliked - Movies marked as not watched - Movies added to watchlists - Movie preferences and in-app interactions **Technical Information** Our infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device types, operating system information, and error logs to maintain application security and functionality. ## 3. How We Use Information We may use collected information to: - Create and manage user accounts - Save movie preferences and watchlists - Generate personalized movie recommendations - Improve recommendation relevance over time - Maintain and improve application functionality - Diagnose technical issues - Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access - Comply with applicable legal obligations Movie preferences may be analyzed to generate recommendations tailored to individual interests. ## 4. Third-Party Services Movipiq may use third-party service providers to support essential application features. These providers may assist with user authentication, data storage, and movie information retrieval. Information is shared with service providers only when necessary to deliver the relevant functionality. Movie descriptions, posters, and metadata may be obtained from external movie database services. Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and data processing practices. ## 5. Data Security We aim to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Secure communication methods are used when transmitting information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Personal information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the application services or comply with applicable legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data. Account deletion includes removal of account information, stored movie preferences, and watchlist data, except for information that must be retained to comply with legal obligations. Account deletion requests can be submitted through the account management section of the App or through the contact channel provided below. ## 7. Your Privacy Rights Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to: - Request access to their personal information - Request correction of inaccurate data - Request deletion of personal information - Obtain information about how their data is processed - Object to certain data processing activities - Exercise other rights granted under applicable privacy laws Users may contact us to exercise these rights. ## 8.
